# Affective Lighting and Wall of Truth

Added November 6, 2026 from the artist’s account, portfolio screenshots, three supplied photographs, and `WALL OF TRUTH POC.mov`.

## Affective Lighting

- Begun in 2024. Original portfolio title: **Chroma: Affective Lighting**.
- The artist describes this as his first investigation into translating rendered emotional states into environmental objects: a ready-made smart light with a clay structure built around it, using American Emotions data.
- The supplied portfolio lists aluminum, LEDs, clay, polymer paint, data inputs, time, and variable dimensions.
- The site uses the illuminated-room photograph and the photograph in ambient light. Full compositions are preserved, exported as WebP with a 2400px longest edge at quality 86. The source files remain outside `public/`.
- The artist connects the experiment to the later ACG installation and New York Emotions. The page does not specify automatic control, update frequency, the exact mapping, or a match between the photographed state and a particular data record; those details have not been supplied.

## Wall of Truth

- Begun in 2024. The artist describes public vulnerability and a form of confessional as central to the project, contributing to Tell Me How You Feel.
- The supplied portfolio describes distributed QR-code stickers leading to an anonymous digital wall. The photograph shows the invitation **Tell Your Story** among layers of chewing gum. Its location is not specified.
- The supplied silent proof-of-concept recording scrolls through entries dated in 2025. It documents the historical interface; the project page does not claim that the recorded wall is a current live feed.
- At the artist’s request, the recording autoplays muted, plays inline, loops, and has no controls.
- Web export: H.264 MP4, original 2874 × 1630 resolution and 60 fps, CRF 18, slow preset, YUV 4:2:0, and fast-start metadata. File size falls from 25,405,884 bytes to 3,885,283 bytes (84.7%). This is perceptual compression, not mathematically lossless. A full-clip FFmpeg comparison reported SSIM 0.999851; text was also inspected against the source at native pixel scale.
- A tested lossless export was 23,972,160 bytes and used the less widely supported High 4:4:4 Predictive profile, so it is not the web source.
